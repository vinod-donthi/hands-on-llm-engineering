# LinkedIn Update Guide

> [← Job Readiness](README.md) · Update incrementally; don't wait until Week 8

---

## Headline (220 chars max)

**Formula:** Role target + proof + stack

Examples:

- `AI Engineer | Building RAG + LangGraph agents | FastAPI · MCP · RAGAS · Azure`
- `Software Engineer → LLM Systems | Multi-provider AI platforms · Production eval pipelines`

Avoid: "Passionate about AI" with no concrete stack.

---

## About section (3 short paragraphs)

1. **Background** — years of SWE, domains you know (cloud, data, backend).
2. **What you're building now** — 2–3 sentences on curriculum projects with links to GitHub.
3. **What you're looking for** — AI Engineer / LLM Platform / Applied AI roles.

## Post templates (adapt from week portfolio docs)

Each week includes `portfolio/linkedin-notes.md` with draft posts. General patterns:

### "Built X" post

```
I just shipped [Project Name] — [one sentence problem].

Stack: [3–4 technologies]
Result: [metric — faithfulness, latency, cost]

What I learned:
• [Insight 1 — e.g. hybrid search beat pure vector on our corpus]
• [Insight 2 — e.g. eval in CI caught a chunking regression]

[GitHub link]
```

### "Lesson learned" post (high engagement)

Pick one failure from [week-03/theory/rag-failure-modes.md](../week-03/theory/rag-failure-modes.md) or agent debugging — explain symptom → root cause → fix.

## Activity cadence

- Week 3–4: 1 post (RAG or agents)
- Week 6: 1 post (eval / production)
- Week 8: 1 capstone announcement + pin to Featured

Quality over quantity — 3 strong posts beat 10 vague ones.
